How this connects to AVNT

The project

Playverse develops and publishes games on Roblox. Super League Soccer is one of its projects. The studio reports a peak of 40,000 simultaneous players for the game. That figure describes the game’s overall reach, not my individual contribution. Studio overview.

My responsibilities

Project coordination
Working on priorities and schedules with the wider team.
Brand and communications
Contributing to how the project appears and communicates through its social channels.
Partnerships
Supporting the project’s work with partners and its brand relationships.

Why it interests me

I’m interested in the organization behind the game: how a team decides what to work on, communicates with players, and keeps a project moving. Those questions connect directly to my interest in studying management.
