Who I am

I’m a senior at Warren Township High School, Class of 2027, from Chicago. I have been Student Council President since freshman year, which means that for four years I have been the person the class turns to when something needs to happen.

Outside school I am president of an independent Black Student Union that draws students from four Lake County districts, and I project-manage FIFA Super Soccer, the FIFA-licensed Roblox football game that began as Super League Soccer and has passed 1.5 billion visits. I have been a volunteer manager for Juneteenth Lake County since 2020. I founded a brand, AVNT, and built the software that runs it. When my class needed a website for senior year, I built that too.

I don’t think of these as separate hobbies. They are the same instinct pointed at different rooms: when something is missing, I make it, and then I make sure it keeps running.

What pulls me in

I’m drawn to the moment a group becomes a thing: a class with a place to find out what’s happening, a union with sixty events behind it, a team that has a way of working.

Technology is how I usually get there, because a website or a tool is the fastest way to give a group somewhere to stand. Business is how I make sure it keeps running. Design is how I make it clear. Leadership is the part where someone has to go first.

Beyond school

The Black Student Union I lead is independent, which is the point: it exists so students from four districts can find each other without waiting for a school to organise it. We rent out bowling alleys and restaurants for kickoffs and big meetings, and we have had state representatives and senators come to speak to students. I am also involved with Black Abolition Movement For The Mind.

I also carry real responsibilities at home. That is part of why I care so much about building things that keep running without me.

What I’ve learned

Shipping is a smaller part of the job than I expected. Keeping something accurate, moderated and alive after launch is most of it.

People will help if you make it easy: give them a role, a form, a log of what happened, and they show up. Almost every project here got better the moment it stopped being only mine.

What’s next

I plan to study business and management. The work I already do, running a licensed game’s schedule, a brand’s operations and a union’s calendar, is management without the degree. I want the degree, and I intend to go on to an MBA or a Master of Accounting.
