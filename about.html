Who I am

I’m a senior at Warren Township High School, in the Class of 2027, and most of what I do outside class comes down to one habit: when something is missing, I try to make it.

That has meant a website for my class when there wasn’t one, a brand and the tools to run it, and a lot of time organising people, whether in person or in an online community. I don’t think of these as separate hobbies. They are the same instinct pointed at different rooms.

What pulls me in

I’m drawn to the moment a group becomes a thing: a class that has a place to find out what’s happening, a community that has a celebration to gather around, a team that has a way of working.

Technology is how I usually get there, because a website or a tool is the fastest way to give a group somewhere to stand. Business is how I make sure it keeps running. Design is how I make it clear. Leadership is the part where someone has to go first.

How the parts connect

  • LeadershipTaking responsibility for a group and the plan.
  • CommunityMaking a place people want to come back to.
  • BusinessTurning an idea into something that keeps running.
  • TechnologyBuilding the tool instead of waiting for one.
  • DesignMaking all of it clear enough to use.

What I’ve learned

Shipping is a smaller part of the job than I expected. Keeping something accurate, moderated and alive after launch is most of it.

I’ve also learned that people will help if you make it easy: give them a role, a form, a log of what happened, and they show up. Almost every project here got better the moment it stopped being only mine.
