I’m a student who likes starting things and staying with them: a website for my class, a community celebration, a brand and the software behind it, and an online community built around a game.

Inside my world

Organising people around something worth doing.

Most of what I’ve built started with a group that needed a place to gather, a plan, or someone willing to go first. Helping lead my class and running an online community taught me the same lesson: people show up when the work is clear and someone is accountable for it.

Building the thing instead of describing it.

I build and run websites and internal tools: a class site with events, announcements and an admin dashboard, and the operations software behind my own brand. The part I care about most is when someone else logs in and it simply works.

Treating an idea like something that has to keep running.

AVNT started as a name and a logo and became a small operation with its own tools, roles and routines. Business, for me, is the discipline of turning an interest into something that still works when I’m not looking at it.

Making room for people to belong.

From a Juneteenth celebration in Lake County to an online community organised around a game, the projects I care about most are the ones where people find each other. The tools change. The job of making people feel welcome doesn’t.
