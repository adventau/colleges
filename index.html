Inside my world

Being the one who is accountable.

I have been Student Council President since freshman year, and I am president of an independent Black Student Union that has run more than sixty events. Leadership is not the title. It is being the person the plan lands on when it meets real people.

See the leadership work

Building the tool instead of waiting for one.

I built and run the Class of 2027 website my class uses for senior year, the operations software behind my own brand, and a private encrypted messaging app with its own iOS shell. The part I care about most is when someone else logs in and it simply works.

See the technology work

Running things that have to keep running.

I project-manage FIFA Super Soccer, the FIFA-licensed Roblox football game that began as Super League Soccer: more than 1.5 billion visits, studio groups of 4.3 million and 1.9 million members, and a studio X account I grew to 130,000 followers. About twelve hours a week. I founded AVNT and built the systems that run it. I plan to study business and management.

See the business work

Making room for people to belong.

Sixty-plus events as BSU president, from kickoffs at rented bowling alleys to meetings where state representatives and senators came to speak to students. Volunteer manager at Juneteenth Lake County’s 2024 festival at Foss Park. The tools change. The job of making people feel welcome does not.

Making things clear enough to be used.

Design runs through everything else here: the identity for AVNT, the layout of a page, a planner cover for my school. I treat it as a supporting discipline, the way I make the rest of the work legible.
